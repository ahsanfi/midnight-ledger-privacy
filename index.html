Privacy Policy — Midnight Ledger

Last updated: 27 October 2026.

This policy describes how the Midnight Ledger Uploader application (the “App”) handles data when it manages the Midnight Ledger YouTube channel. The App is a private tool used only by the channel owner. It is not offered to the public, and it does not collect data from viewers.

Information the App accesses

Through the YouTube Data API v3, and only after the channel owner grants consent on Google’s consent screen, the App can:

  • upload videos to the Midnight Ledger channel;
  • read video and channel information (titles, descriptions, visibility, and statistics shown to the owner);
  • set custom video thumbnails; and
  • update video details.

The Google scopes requested are youtube.upload and youtube. The App does not access email, contacts, Drive files, search history, or any Google service other than YouTube.

How information is used

Information accessed through the API is used solely to upload and manage videos on the Midnight Ledger channel. Data is not sold, rented, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Data is transmitted only to YouTube and Google, as necessary to perform the actions the owner requested.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored securely on a private server controlled by the channel owner. They are never shared, published, or embedded in public code.

Retention and revocation

Credentials are kept only while the App is in use. The channel owner can revoke the App’s access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access immediately stops the App from acting on the channel.

Children’s privacy

The channel’s content is not directed at children, and the App does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page. Continued use of the App after a change means acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the Midnight Ledger YouTube channel: Midnight Ledger on YouTube.
